# Market Street — Stakeholders

The following stakeholders are involved in or affected by the simulated stock-trading transaction.

## Stakeholder Overview

| Stakeholder | Role in the Transaction | What They Need |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginner Investor** | Initiates and submits a simulated stock trade order | Simple order entry, clear trade details, confirmation, and an easy-to-understand transaction status |
| **Trading System Administrator** | Monitors the application and manages system operations | Accurate transaction records, reliable performance, and visibility into failed or invalid trades |
| **Market Data Provider** | Supplies stock prices and market information used to process simulated trades | Accurate and timely data requests and proper handling of returned market information |
| **Compliance / Audit Reviewer** | Reviews trade history and system records | Traceable records, timestamps, transaction status, and proof that business rules were applied consistently |

## Main Transaction

> A beginner investor submits a simulated stock trade order, and the system validates, records, and returns the transaction status.
